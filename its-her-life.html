It's Her Life

2015

Medium
Acrylic on navy illustration board

Dimensions
22 × 14 in.

Status
Private collection

About the Artwork

It's Her Life was painted in 2015, a few months after Mia was born. Created in acrylic on navy illustration board, the 22 × 14 inch painting places a woman in profile within a field of layered color and flowing movement.

The original was later raffled and now lives in a private collection. Years after the painting was created, it continued to resurface through photographs, conversations and the idea of bringing the artwork onto a mug.

2015 → 2023

Behind the Artwork

From an early social media post in 2015 to the painting finding a new home and inspiring a mug concept years later, these pieces from the archive follow It's Her Life across time.

September 16, 2015

From the 2015 Archive

An early Instagram post documented the painting in progress in September 2015. The original post described the work through tags including acrylic, painting, illustration, woman, hair and fantasy.

Looking back years later, I noted that the painting had been made a few months after Mia was born.

September 18, 2023

A New Home

The original painting was raffled and delivered to its winner. A photograph shared in 2023 shows It's Her Life displayed in its new home.

The image became another chapter in the history of the piece, documenting where the original went after leaving the studio.

2023

The Mug Idea

After the raffle delivery was shared, a comment asked whether copies of the painting might appear on a cup, T-shirt or as a print. I replied that I had plans and shared a mug mockup using the artwork.

The idea stayed in the archive, waiting for the right time to return.
