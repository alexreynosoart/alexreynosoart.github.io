Art Challenges & Exercises

Sometimes the best way to make art is to stop thinking about making it perfect. Try an Everything Art exercise, then explore creative challenges happening around the art community.

Everything Art Exercise #001

DON'T STOP. JUST DRAW. ✍️

Divide one sheet of paper into 2 or 4 sections. Put your pen or pencil down and keep it moving. Don't plan the picture. Let your hand, wrist and arm loosen up and follow the line wherever it wants to go.

KEEP MOVING
NO PLANNING
NO ERASING
NO RESTARTING
DETAILS CAN WAIT
UGLY IS ALLOWED

Loosen the hand. Free the arm. Find the flow.

One sheet. Multiple exercises. Less paper, less waste. Paper version and digital Creative Studio version are coming next.
