Art belongs to everybody.

Everything Art is an international community of artists and art lovers created to inspire, encourage and celebrate creativity in all its forms.

Painting, drawing, photography, sculpture, illustration, design, fashion, poetry, animation, pottery and everything in between have a place here.

Beginners are welcome. Professionals are welcome. Works in progress are welcome.

Why Everything Art Exists

We are here to motivate and encourage one another in EVERYTHING ART.

Creativity should not be intimidating. Everything Art is a place to discover work, share ideas, find inspiration and encourage people to keep creating.

The Future

Something Bigger Is Growing. 🌱

Everything Art began as an online community with a simple belief: artists should encourage artists. The long-term dream is to take that spirit beyond the screen and create new opportunities for artists to create, learn, teach, exhibit and build community together.
