01 / Music Visual Commission

Shelita

HOLA.

A visual world developed around singer-songwriter Shelita's song Hola , combining atmospheric environments, compositing, motion and surreal imagery.

Music Visual Compositing Motion Completed
01

The Song

It started with Hola.

Shelita reached out with her song Hola and an invitation to develop a visual experience around the music.

From there, the project became an exploration of color, atmosphere, movement and romantic, dreamlike imagery , building a world that could live alongside the song rather than simply illustrating it.

02 / Building The World

Start with pieces.
Build a place.

Individual visual elements were layered into an atmospheric environment and then developed further through movement, editing and compositing.

Visual Ingredients
01 / ENVIRONMENT

Set the atmosphere.

Color, landscape and light established the dreamlike visual language of the project.

02 / ELEMENTS

Add the unexpected.

Glowing objects, surreal imagery and individual visual pieces gave the world its personality.

03 / MOTION

Make the world move.

Animation, compositing and editing transformed the still ingredients into a moving visual experience.

03 / In Motion

The environment comes alive.

A still environment becomes a moving backdrop , one of the layers used to construct the final visual.

Animated environment Visual development / Alex Reynoso Art

04 / Behind The Scenes

Layer by layer.

A look inside the process as the individual pieces are combined, adjusted and developed into the finished visual world.

The Result

A visual world for Hola.

The finished project brings together atmospheric landscapes, surreal elements, motion and compositing to create a visual companion to Shelita's music.

The project began with a song. Everything else grew from there.
