FORMAT SUPPORT

Available now: JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP.

Building toward: AVIF, GIF, BMP, TIFF, HEIC/HEIF, PDF and camera RAW including DNG, CR2/CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF and RW2.

Formats only appear as conversion choices when the Studio can process them reliably in your browser.

Transparent PNGs are kept as PNG so transparency is not lost. JPG/WebP images are recompressed as WebP.
