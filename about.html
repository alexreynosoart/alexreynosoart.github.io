About Alex

I'm Alex Reynoso, a visual artist working across illustration, painting, animation, video and digital media.

I've spent years moving between traditional art and new technology, always looking for different ways to bring an idea to life. Sometimes that means a painting. Sometimes it's animation. Sometimes it's a dancing animal that probably shouldn't be dancing.

Alex Reynoso Art is where all of those worlds meet.
