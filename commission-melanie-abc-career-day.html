01 / The Book

26 careers.
One illustrated book.

ABC Career Day introduces young readers to different careers from A to Z. Each letter pairs a job with a short description written for a younger audience, while the illustrations turn each occupation into its own scene.

The English edition is currently being illustrated. The pages move through layout, rough artwork, cleanup, client review and revisions before they are ready for the book.

03 / How A Page Gets Built

Before a page is finished, it goes through a few versions. The early layout is there to see the idea, composition and progress. Then the drawing takes over.
