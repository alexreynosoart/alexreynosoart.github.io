Before you apply

Everything Art shares opportunities as a resource for artists. Deadlines, eligibility, fees and program details can change. Always confirm the current requirements with the organization offering the opportunity before submitting an application or payment.

We prioritize original sources, official government programs and established arts organizations whenever possible.
